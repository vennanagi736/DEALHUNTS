# DEALHUNTS

## Full Stack Product Comparison & Inventory Management Platform

DEALHUNTS is a full-stack web application designed to simplify product discovery, comparison, and inventory management. The platform provides separate workflows for administrators, vendors, and users to manage products, inventory, and product information efficiently.

---

## Features

### Admin Module
- Admin dashboard for managing application data
- Product master data management
- Category management
- Brand management
- Variant management
- Color management
- Product specification management

### Vendor Module
- Vendor product management
- Product selection from master catalog
- Inventory management
- Stock tracking
- Pricing management
- Offer and warranty management
- Product image management

### User Module
- User authentication
- Product browsing
- Product comparison
- Product details viewing
